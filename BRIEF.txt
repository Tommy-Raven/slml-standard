INSTITUTIONAL BRIEF
STRUCTURAL LEGITIMACY MANIFEST LANGUAGE (SLML)
===========================================

STATUS
------

This document is NON-NORMATIVE. It does not define, modify, or extend
the SLML standard.

SLML v0.1 is PRE-RELEASE. The repository does not currently provide an
independently verifiable immutable release. No compliance, certification,
or release-integrity claim may rely on the current repository state.

For the authoritative repository status and release-readiness conditions,
see the root README.txt. Any reference in this brief to a future
implementation path, release branch, tag, hash, metadata, validator, or
immutability mechanism is preparatory and does not establish a release.

DOCUMENTATION CLAIM RESTRICTION
-------------------------------

Until release readiness is complete, repository documentation, examples,
metadata descriptions, and contributor-facing materials MUST NOT assert
that anything is:

- released
- certified under SLML
- an immutable standard
- a cryptographically sealed release
- independently verifiable compliance

Use qualified terms such as PRE-RELEASE, DRAFT, STAGING, or RELEASE
CANDIDATE instead. The restricted phrases may appear only when explicitly
discussing this prohibition or a future release-readiness condition.

PURPOSE
-------

The Structural Legitimacy Manifest Language (SLML) is a proposed,
standardized, binary-output, deterministic evaluation framework intended
to assess whether a system is structurally admissible to claim
user-serving legitimacy.

SLML addresses a specific institutional gap: existing mechanisms such as
ethics guidelines, audits, compliance programs, and fact-checking systems
primarily evaluate intent, outcomes, or content after harm has occurred.
SLML is intended to evaluate structure at design time.

SCOPE OF EVALUATION
-------------------

SLML is intended to evaluate ONLY structural admissibility, including:

- explicit ownership
- explicit consent
- obligation directionality
- inconvenience symmetry

It is not intended to evaluate moral intent, legality, popularity,
utility, benefit aggregation, outcome quality, harm severity, or policy
compliance. It produces no scores, rankings, or gradients.

NON-AUTHORIZATION CLARIFICATION
-------------------------------

SLML does not authorize or permit action, license or certify systems,
validate or endorse claims, approve or sanction behavior, confer
legitimacy, legality, or compliance, or substitute for governance, law,
or oversight. No SLML output constitutes permission, approval, or
authority of any kind.

LIMITATIONS
-----------

SLML makes no claim about ethical sufficiency, legal compliance, social
desirability, fairness outcomes, or policy alignment. Institutions remain
responsible for interpretation, governance, and decision-making.

END OF BRIEF
